# Heistathon at the Wizard's Keep

## Theme

You are a mischievous creature with a plan to steal as many of the wizard's magic items as you can.

You've managed to obtain a position in the wizard's employ, but his golems search you whenever you leave the Wizard's Keep, so you'll have to sneak out the loot through more indirect means...

You've come up with a heist plan:

1. Feed the magic items to the wizard's pets.
2. Take the pets for walks.
3. Profit!

The wizard has 5 pets, and each pet has a favorite kind of magic item that it likes to eat.

| Pet Name | Pet Description | Favorite Magic Item Snack |
| - | - | - |
| Uxivarg | Unicorn-Dragon | Potion |
| Baast | Two-Headed Snail-Cat | Diamond |
| Apollo | Many-Legged Puppy | Amulet |
| Pikamander | Hopping Fire Elemental | Wand |
| Rocky | Granite Bird | Idol |

## Objective

Steal as many magic items as you can by the end of the game.

Each numbered card in your score pile is worth as many points as its number (the 4 of potions is worth 4 points).

Cards in your hand and cards lying around the Wizard's Keep at the end of the game are not scored.

Players can also get bonus points for having the most 1s or the most wild cards in their score piles at the end of the game.

## Components

### Walk Decks

Each player has their own deck of 5 walk cards, one walk card for each of the 5 pets.

### Magic Item Deck

The magic item deck has 115 cards, 23 cards for each of the 5 different kinds of magic items.

Each kind of magic item has cards numbered 1 through 7 in triplicate, as well as 2 wild cards.

### Pet Cards

There is a double-sided pet card for each pet.

That card shows the rule that determines what makes that pet sick.

Magic item cards stacked face-up next to a pet have been eaten by that pet.

## Setup

Each player shuffles their own walk deck.

Shuffle the magic item deck, then take a number of cards away from that deck to start a discard pile.

| # of Players | # of Cards to Discard During Setup | # of Cards Remaining |
| - | - | - |
| 2 | 35 | 80 |
| 3 | 20 | 95 |
| 4 | 5 | 110 |

Put the remaining magic item deck in the center of the play area.

Arrange the pet cards in a circle around the magic item deck.

For your first game, put all the pets with their "3-straight" side face up.

Each player draws 2 walk cards from their personal walk deck and 5 magic item cards from the magic item deck.

Whichever player is ready first may take the first turn, after which turns proceed clockwise.

## Sick Pets

A pet gets sick if the face-up magic item cards in front of it include cards that satisfy its sick condition. The order of the cards within a pet's pile of eaten cards does not matter when determining whether that pet has gotten sick.

Example: If a pet has the "3-straight" rule and the cards it has eaten are numbered 1, 4, 4, and 5, that pet is OK because those cards do not contain 3 numbers in a row.

Example: If a pet has the "3-straight" rule and the cards it has eaten are numbered 6, 2, 5, and 7, that pet gets sick because they include 5-6-7, regardless of the order in which those cards appear within its pile of eaten cards.

When determining whether a pet gets sick, wild cards take on whatever whole-number value they need from 1 through 7 to make the pet sick.

Example: If a pet has the "3-straight" rule and the cards it has eaten are 3, wild, and 7, that pet is OK because those cards do not make a straight, no matter what number the wild card takes on.

Example: If a pet has the "3-straight" rule and the cards it has eaten are 7, wild, and 5, that pet gets sick because the wild card could take on 6 as its number.

Note that the sick pet rules do not consider which kind of magic item is depicted on those cards.

When a pet gets sick, discard all the magic item cards that it has eaten and 5 cards from the top of the magic item deck.

## On Your Turn

Each turn you must choose one of the following 2 options: Feed the pets, or take a pet for a walk.

### Feeding the pets

If you choose to feed the pets, you'll play 2 magic item cards.

1. The first magic item card you play must be played face up next to the matching pet.
2. The second magic item card you play is played face down between the pet that ate your first card and one of its neighbors. This can add to a face-down pile between those 2 pets or start a face-down pile if there are not currently any cards between them. The face-down card can be any type of magic item.

After playing both cards, check whether the pet that received a face-up card has become sick. If it has, resolve this according to the "Sick Pets" rules before continuing.

### Walking a Pet

If you choose to walk a pet instead, follow these steps:

1. Reveal one of the walk cards from your hand.
2. If there is at least one magic item card in a face-down pile adjacent to that pet, you may reveal the top card of one of those adjacent piles and add it to the face-up pile for the pet that you're walking. If there are no adjacent face-down magic item cards, or you choose not to reveal one, proceed to step 3.
   - 2a. If the pet is now sick, resolve this by discarding all the magic item cards that the pet has eaten and the top 5 cards of the magic item deck. Your walk then ends without you collecting any magic item cards into your score pile.
   - 2b. If the pet is not sick, repeat step 2.
3. A walk reaches this step because the adjacent magic item piles ran out without the pet getting sick or because the walking player chose to stop before the pet got sick. Either way, the walking player collects all the face-up magic item cards eaten by this pet into their score pile.

Each player gets only 1 chance to walk each pet during the game. Whether a walk ends with the pet getting sick or with the player collecting magic items into their score pile, the walk card that the player used should be left face up near their score pile as a reminder that they have already walked that pet.

### End of Turn

Once you've completed the chosen action, end your turn by drawing cards until you're back up to your starting hand size: 2 walk cards and 5 magic item cards.

## End of the Game

The game ends when either one of the following 2 conditions is met:

- Heist Complete! If all players have played all their walk cards, then all the thievery is complete and the game ends.
- The Wizard Banishes Everyone! If the magic item deck runs out, the wizard realizes that his magic items have gone missing and he won't let anyone walk his pets anymore.

No matter how the game ends, discard all the cards from the players' hands, all the cards currently eaten by the pets, and all the face-down cards between the pets. The only cards that can score points are those that have already been collected into a player's score pile when the game ends.

## Scoring

If there is one player who has collected more wild cards than any other, award that player the "most wilds" bonus card, which is worth 10 points. Wild cards are not worth any points other than contributing to that bonus.

If one player has collected more 1s than any other, award that player the "most 1s" bonus card, which is worth 10 points.

Each player then adds up the numbers on the magic item cards they've collected, along with any bonuses they've been awarded, to determine their final score.
